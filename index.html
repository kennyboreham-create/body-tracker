Hormones & Joint Health

1. Caloric Deficit with High Protein

Fat loss requires burning more calories than you consume. Keeping protein consistently around 180–200g ensures body fat loss rather than muscle degradation.

2. 3-Day Compound Lifting

Full-body compound movements trigger maximum muscle protein synthesis without overtaxing your central nervous system before game day.

3. Hockey & Energy Management

Thursday night hockey serves as an intense anaerobic workout. Carbs are elevated slightly on Thursday afternoon to maximize muscle glycogen for peak performance.

5 Daily Meal Plans (~2200-2400 kcal, ~180-200g Protein)

Option 1: The Classic Hockey Athlete Plan

  • Breakfast: 3 eggs + 3 egg whites scramble with spinach, 1 cup cooked oatmeal with 1/2 cup blueberries (42g P, 450 kcal)
  • Lunch: 6 oz grilled chicken breast, 1 cup brown rice, broccoli with 1 tsp olive oil (50g P, 520 kcal)
  • Afternoon: 1 cup 2% Greek yogurt + 1 scoop whey protein + 1 banana (40g P, 350 kcal)
  • Dinner: 7 oz 93/7 extra lean ground turkey, 1 medium sweet potato, side salad (50g P, 580 kcal)
  • Night: 1 cup low-fat cottage cheese or whey shake (28g P, 180 kcal)

Option 2: Steak & Salmon Strength Focus

  • Breakfast: Protein smoothie (2 scoops whey, 1 cup almond milk, 1 cup frozen berries, 1 tbsp peanut butter) (50g P, 480 kcal)
  • Lunch: 7 oz sirloin steak (trimmed), 1 medium baked potato, asparagus (52g P, 550 kcal)
  • Afternoon: 2 hard-boiled eggs + 2 slices Ezekiel bread with 3 oz deli turkey (32g P, 340 kcal)
  • Dinner: 6 oz Atlantic salmon fillet, 1 cup quinoa, roasted zucchini (42g P, 580 kcal)
  • Night: 1 scoop Micellar Casein protein in water (25g P, 120 kcal)

Option 3: Quick Prep & On-The-Go

  • Breakfast: 1.5 cups egg white omelet with peppers and 2 oz lean ham + 2 slices whole wheat toast (40g P, 400 kcal)
  • Lunch: 2 cans (10 oz total) tuna packed in water mixed with 1 tbsp mayo, whole wheat wrap, apple (55g P, 490 kcal)
  • Afternoon: Protein bar (20g P) + 1 cup Greek yogurt (20g P, 360 kcal)
  • Dinner: 7 oz grilled chicken thighs (skinless), 1 cup white rice, green beans (46g P, 560 kcal)
  • Night: 1 cup cottage cheese with 10 almonds (28g P, 220 kcal)

Option 4: Thursday Game-Day Carbo-Focus Plan

  • Breakfast: 3 whole eggs + 1 cup oats cooked in milk + honey (38g P, 520 kcal)
  • Lunch (3-4h Pre-Game): 7 oz chicken breast, 1.5 cups jasmine rice, steamed carrots (52g P, 610 kcal)
  • Pre-Hockey Snack (1.5h before): 1 scoop whey + 1 rice cake with jam (26g P, 220 kcal)
  • Post-Game Recovery: 7 oz extra lean ground beef bowl with white rice & salsa (52g P, 580 kcal)
  • Night: Casein Shake or Greek Yogurt (25g P, 140 kcal)

Option 5: High Power Pork & Turkey Protocol

  • Breakfast: 4 scrambled eggs, 3 slices turkey bacon, 1 orange (35g P, 420 kcal)
  • Lunch: 7 oz pork tenderloin roast, 1 medium sweet potato, roasted broccoli (48g P, 480 kcal)
  • Afternoon: 1 scoop whey + 1/2 cup oatmeal blended smoothie (32g P, 310 kcal)
  • Dinner: 8 oz turkey burgers (no bun), large garden salad with avocado, olive oil (54g P, 600 kcal)
  • Night: 1 cup Greek yogurt + chia seeds (25g P, 180 kcal)
